Years are when a name, border, or feature first and last appears on an up-to-date map. Published maps often lag one or two years behind events, so treat the result as the year the map depicts, and allow that it may have been printed a little later. Boundary years count as compatible with both states. Coverage starts at 1850. Country outlines are from Natural Earth (public domain).
